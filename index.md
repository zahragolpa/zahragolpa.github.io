# Welcome!


Hi!  
My name is Zahra Golpayegani, and I am an AI researcher.

At Huawei, I focus on **optimizing** large language models (LLMs) for Ascend NPU chips, improving performance during both **training** and **inference**. My work involves studying state-of-the-art LLMs, **profiling** them to identify bottlenecks, designing and implementing **new architectures**, using modern frameworks to **train and evaluate** models, and exploring **parallelization strategies** to optimize execution.

Recently, I have been working on **reinforcement learning for LLMs** using frameworks such as <a href="https://github.com/verl-project/verl"><b>VeRL</b></a> to train **multi-turn agentic models** for long-horizon tasks. VeRL integrates with the <a href="https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm"><b>vLLM</b></a> serving engine and its Ascend plugin, <a href="https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm-ascend"><b>vLLM Ascend</b></a>, which are used during RL training, e.g., in rollout generation.  

In earlier projects, I worked with <a href="https://github.com/NVIDIA/Megatron-LM"><b>Megatron-LM</b></a> for large-scale LLM training with billions of parameters, and used <a href="https://github.com/hiyouga/LlamaFactory"><b>LLaMA-Factory</b></a> to fine-tune LLaMA-based models.

For more details, check out my <a href="/resume">CV</a>.

Previously, I worked as a Machine Learning Engineer at **Zetane Systems Inc.**, contributing to two key products: **Protector**, which evaluates the **robustness** of commercial computer vision models against **adversarial attacks** and real-world transformations, and <a href="https://get.zetaforge.com/"><b>ZetaForge</b></a>, a visual **AI pipeline builder** that enables users to create and customize **complex AI workflows** through an intuitive drag-and-drop interface.

I hold a Bachelor of Science in **Computer Science** from Amirkabir University of Technology and a Master’s in **Systems Engineering** from **Concordia University**. During my Master’s, I received a **Mitacs Accelerate Explore award** to work on testing, validation, and quality assurance of computer vision models and datasets in partnership with Zetane. This experience marked the beginning of my journey at Zetane, first as a part-time Researcher and later as a full-time Machine Learning Engineer.
